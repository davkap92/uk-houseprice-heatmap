2025 house prices per m² in England and Wales

2,111 postcode districts · 390,115 matched sales · January 2026 pounds

Data and method

Huw Diprose links HM Land Registry sale prices to domestic EPC floor areas. Each district's price is the total adjusted sale value divided by total floor area. The source includes districts with at least 10 matched sales in 2025.
